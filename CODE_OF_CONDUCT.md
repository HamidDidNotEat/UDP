# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience level or background.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks or publishing other people's private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in this project's issues, pull requests or discussions breaks these rules, report it to the maintainer ([HamidDidNotEat](https://github.com/HamidDidNotEat)). For anything sensitive, use the private **Report a vulnerability** form in the repository's **Security** tab and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so.
